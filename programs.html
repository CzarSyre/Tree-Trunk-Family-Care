Our Programs

Transforming lives through faith, education and empowerment.

What We Do

```

Tree Trunk Family Care serves vulnerable families and children through holistic programs that address spiritual, educational and social needs.

Family Preservation

Strengthening families through counselling, parenting support, spiritual guidance and economic empowerment.

Education Support

Providing school fees, scholastic materials, mentorship and educational opportunities for vulnerable children.

Community Empowerment

Equipping communities with practical skills, training and opportunities for sustainable development.

Child & Youth Mentorship

Raising young leaders through discipleship, life skills and leadership development programs.

Evangelism & Discipleship

Sharing the Gospel and nurturing spiritual growth among children, youth and families.

Emergency Response

Providing relief and support to families facing crises and unexpected hardships.
```
